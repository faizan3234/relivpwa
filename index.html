    ⚠️ Clinical Screening Notice:

    Some of your symptoms can occur with hormonal conditions such as PCOS. This app cannot diagnose PCOS. Consider discussing your symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional, especially if you have irregular periods or other persistent concerns.

    🌿 Lifestyle & Cycle Guidance:
    • Skincare: Use Salicylic Acid (BHA) to unclog pores and Benzoyl Peroxide to treat inflamed hormonal acne cysts. Avoid squeezing deep jawline cysts.
    • Diet: Prioritize low-GI, whole foods, spearmint tea (anti-androgenetic effects), and healthy omega-3 fats.
    • Sleep & Stress: Aim for 8 hours of sleep. High cortisol triggers androgen release, worsening breakouts.
    🩺 Questions for your Doctor:

    "Could my chin breakouts and period patterns be linked to insulin resistance or excess androgens?"

    🔬 Tests a Clinician May Consider:

    Pelvic ultrasound (to check ovaries), blood panel for Free Testosterone, fasting insulin, DHEAS, and thyroid levels (TSH).
